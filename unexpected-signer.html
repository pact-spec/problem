unexpected-signer

https://pact-spec.github.io/problem/unexpected-signer

HTTP status
422
Defined by
The reference implementation. Revision -01 does not list it.
Section named
Section 5 of revision -01

When it is returned

An object is signed by a party that may not sign it. There are two cases: a contract that carries a signature from a party that is neither its Buyer nor its Seller, and a Challenge signed by the contract's Seller.

The second case is a choice of the implementation. Section 7.5 of revision -01 lets any party submit a Challenge.

Recorded examples

  • Event 33 of the recorded run, status 422: “the contract carries a signature from a party that is neither its Buyer nor its Seller”
  • Test vector reject/06-challenge-by-seller, status 422: “a Seller cannot challenge its own Delivery”

These identifiers belong to revision -01 of draft-laxsharma-pact, an individual Internet-Draft that later revisions have superseded. Later revisions do not use this prefix.

The implementation described is the reference implementation at tag v0.1.2 of pact-spec/spec.
