PACT problem types, revision -01

proof-nonconformant

https://pact-spec.github.io/problem/proof-nonconformant

HTTP status
422
Defined by
The reference implementation. Revision -01 does not list it.
Section named
Section 7.5 of revision -01

When it is returned

A Challenge's proof does not conform to the contract. It names a verification profile other than the contract's, does not commit to the instrument the contract committed to, or lacks results_hash under the acceptance profile.

Recorded examples

  • Event 58 of the recorded run, status 422: “the proof does not commit to the instrument the contract committed to”

These identifiers belong to revision -01 of draft-laxsharma-pact, an individual Internet-Draft that later revisions have superseded. Later revisions do not use this prefix.

The implementation described is the reference implementation at tag v0.1.2 of pact-spec/spec.
